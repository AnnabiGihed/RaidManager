## Goal (draft)

The first usable version of RaidManager: players and raid leaders in a Discord community plan Warmane WoW 3.3.5a raids
through the website, the Discord bot, the addon and the desktop companion, with automatic character synchronization
and a reliable answer to whether a character can join a raid at its scheduled start.

## Acceptance criteria (draft)

The version 1 acceptance journeys of the product scope, each demonstrated end to end:

1. A new player pairs one companion, visits characters across two WoW accounts, approves each new character at the
   next website sign-in, and sees its last sync and raid saves.
2. A player offers multiple specializations through either channel, saves a preset, and sees one consistent
   confirmed, tentative, late, or declined response on both channels.
3. A character saved today can join a raid after its reset, but can't be offered for a raid before that reset. An
   unknown save requests synchronization and never appears as confirmed available.
4. An officer creates a recurring raid, reviews candidates and lockout reasons, publishes one character per player,
   and later replaces an absent participant from the bench without rebuilding the raid.
5. A changed raid time or fresh lockout snapshot triggers a new verdict and alerts the affected player and officer.
   The officer can record a reasoned exception for unknown data, but not for a confirmed active lockout.
6. At raid time, the officer checks attendance and exports the current roster to the addon for in-game invitations.
7. Before raid time, the player receives one reminder and can see boss assignments; afterwards, the officer can
   review attendance history and gear-readiness warnings for future planning.

The repository's quality gates pass on the released version (build, tests, coverage, documentation, review and
SonarCloud), and the release checks of the stabilization sprint are recorded below.
