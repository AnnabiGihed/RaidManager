## Goal (draft)

Officers build, publish and run rosters through raid night, completing the version 1 product scope.

## Acceptance criteria (draft)

1. An officer creates a recurring raid, reviews candidates and lockout reasons, publishes one character per player, and
   later replaces an absent participant from the bench without rebuilding the raid (journey 4).
2. At raid time, the officer checks attendance and exports the current roster to the addon for in-game invitations
   (journey 6).
3. Before raid time, the player receives one reminder and can see boss assignments; afterwards, the officer can review
   attendance history and gear-readiness warnings for future planning (journey 7).

The repository's quality gates pass on the released version, and the stabilization sprint records its release checks
below.
