## Goal (draft)

Players sign in with Discord, see their community, pair the desktop companion, and get their WoW characters captured,
uploaded, reviewed and shown on their profile. It is the first release to be deployed.

## Acceptance criteria (draft)

1. A new player pairs one companion, visits characters across two WoW accounts, approves each new character at the next
   website sign-in, and sees its last sync and raid saves (version 1 journey 1).
2. An officer's permissions come from the community's roles, and losing the Discord role removes them.
3. The deployment stores and delivers its secrets as the decision of #103 records, so the release can be deployed.

The repository's quality gates pass on the released version, and the stabilization sprint records its release checks
below.
